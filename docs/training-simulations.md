| Mode | Learn by playing |

## Source and gameplay boundaries

- Enemies and lore can refer to real malware and vulnerabilities. Cards and boss reports describe broad symptoms, exposure and impacts; remediation links take you to primary vendor or government guidance. The simulations do not execute tools, contain exploit instructions or target any systems.
- Health, shield, damage and energy are balance numbers, not security measurements. A cited, observed tool rating can add one point of in-game health on eligible cards. Unrated tools are neutral; a customer review score is not a safety rating.
- Packet Duel adapts the rules of the GameSync card game documented in <a href="https://github.com/Herbertofury/Athena" title="Open Athena official download or service page" aria-label="Open Athena official download or service page" data-inta-brand="app-athena"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/99f4edb33272b70febb8.png" alt="Athena official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Athena’s extracted GameSync engine](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Athena/tree/main/engines/card-game). It independently implements the mechanics. No GPL game-engine code or upstream game artwork is copied. Tool logos use <a href="https://herbertofury.github.io/INTA/" title="Open INTA official download or service page" aria-label="Open INTA official download or service page" data-inta-brand="app-inta"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/70ad9f0981b05341142b.png" alt="INTA official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [INTA](https://herbertofury.github.io/INTA/)’s existing attributed catalog.
- Games and checklist work offline in the built Hub. Opening a source link intentionally leaves the simulation. Saves belong to this browser; no account, telemetry, cloud save or background process is required.
