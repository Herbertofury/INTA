### 🚀 Fix a PC that will not boot

**Record the startup error and confirm disk health and <a href="https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/security/operating-system-security/data-protection/bitlocker/" title="Open BitLocker official download or service page" aria-label="Open BitLocker official download or service page" data-inta-brand="app-bitlocker"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4538691198fe0ea7526e.png" alt="BitLocker official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [BitLocker](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/security/operating-system-security/data-protection/bitlocker/) recovery access before attempting repairs.**

1. Check power, attached devices and the selected boot disk; stop if storage is failing.
2. Open <a href="https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/manufacture/desktop/windows-recovery-environment--windows-re--technical-reference" title="Open Windows Recovery Environment official download or service page" aria-label="Open Windows Recovery Environment official download or service page" data-inta-brand="windows-recovery"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4538691198fe0ea7526e.png" alt="Windows Recovery Environment official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Windows Recovery Environment](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/manufacture/desktop/windows-recovery-environment--windows-re--technical-reference) and run <a href="https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/startup-boot/startup-repair" title="Open Startup Repair official download or service page" aria-label="Open Startup Repair official download or service page" data-inta-brand="app-startup-repair"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4538691198fe0ea7526e.png" alt="Startup Repair official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Startup Repair](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/startup-boot/startup-repair) for the identified installation when appropriate.
3. Follow the matching recovery branch, verify a normal boot and open priority files before closing the job.

| **A broad known startup failure may have a cloud remedy** | Check supported <a href="https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/configuration/quick-machine-recovery/?tabs=csp" title="Open Quick machine recovery official download or service page" aria-label="Open Quick machine recovery official download or service page" data-inta-brand="quick-machine-recovery"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4538691198fe0ea7526e.png" alt="Quick machine recovery official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Quick machine recovery](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/configuration/quick-machine-recovery/?tabs=csp) availability, network access and organization policy in the recovery interface. | A remedy is offered and tested, or ordinary recovery continues without assuming a cloud fix exists. |

### ✅ Finish the job

- [ ] The device boots normally twice and the user can open priority files.
- [ ] The intended boot disk and encryption recovery remain available.

**Change route when:** The disk fails, disconnects or contains the only unprotected data copy. · Recovery keys or the identity of the target installation are uncertain.

### 🎯 Go straight to a specific fix

- **[Find a disk missing from recovery media](#fix-boot-installer-cannot-see-storage)** — Firmware sees the disk but [Windows](https://www.microsoft.com/windows) installation or recovery media does not.
- **[Identify the Windows volume inside recovery](#fix-boot-check-recovery-drive-letters)** — An offline repair cannot find [Windows](https://www.microsoft.com/windows) or appears to target the wrong drive.
- **<a href="https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/security/operating-system-security/data-protection/bitlocker/" title="Open BitLocker official download or service page" aria-label="Open BitLocker official download or service page" data-inta-brand="app-bitlocker"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4538691198fe0ea7526e.png" alt="BitLocker official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Find the matching BitLocker key](#fix-boot-recovery-key-does-not-match)** — A stored recovery key is rejected or several similarly named device records exist.

> [!NOTE]
> **Start here.** Record the exact screen and failure stage. Obtain the device’s recovery-key access path before any firmware, TPM, or boot changes.

## Procedure

Work in order, record the result of each step, and change route when the evidence calls for it.

<a id="step-01"></a>

### 01 · Identify the failure stage

- [ ] Check whether the system powers on, completes firmware POST, detects its drive, reaches [Windows](https://www.microsoft.com/windows) Boot Manager, or fails after the [Windows](https://www.microsoft.com/windows) logo. Disconnect only nonessential peripherals. A missing drive or hardware error goes to hardware or failing-drive diagnosis.

<a id="step-02"></a>

### 02 · Protect data and encryption access

- [ ] Confirm the <a href="https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/security/operating-system-security/data-protection/bitlocker/" title="Open BitLocker official download or service page" aria-label="Open BitLocker official download or service page" data-inta-brand="app-bitlocker"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4538691198fe0ea7526e.png" alt="BitLocker official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [BitLocker](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/security/operating-system-security/data-protection/bitlocker/) recovery key is available through the customer’s approved account or management system. Never publish it in the wiki or ticket attachments visible to unrelated staff. Do not clear the TPM, initialize the disk, or reinstall over the only data copy.

<a id="step-03"></a>

### 03 · Enter <a href="https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/manufacture/desktop/windows-recovery-environment--windows-re--technical-reference" title="Open Windows Recovery Environment official download or service page" aria-label="Open Windows Recovery Environment official download or service page" data-inta-brand="windows-recovery"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4538691198fe0ea7526e.png" alt="Windows Recovery Environment official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Windows Recovery Environment](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/manufacture/desktop/windows-recovery-environment--windows-re--technical-reference)

- [ ] Use the built-in recovery environment or official <a href="https://www.microsoft.com/software-download/windows11" title="Open Windows 11 installation media official download or service page" aria-label="Open Windows 11 installation media official download or service page" data-inta-brand="microsoft-windows11-media"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4538691198fe0ea7526e.png" alt="Windows 11 installation media official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Windows installation media](https://www.microsoft.com/software-download/windows11) and select Repair my PC. Check the actual volume layout and encryption status before working on an offline installation; drive letters can differ from the normal [Windows](https://www.microsoft.com/windows) session.

**CMD or <a href="https://github.com/PowerShell/PowerShell/releases/latest" title="Open PowerShell official download or service page" aria-label="Open PowerShell official download or service page" data-inta-brand="powershell"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4538691198fe0ea7526e.png" alt="PowerShell official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [PowerShell](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/powershell/) · Restart into the [Windows](https://www.microsoft.com/windows) advanced startup options when the machine still boots and the owner has approved the restart.:**

```text
shutdown /r /o /t 0
```

> **Before running:** Immediately restarts the PC. Save work, confirm the correct device, make <a href="https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/security/operating-system-security/data-protection/bitlocker/" title="Open BitLocker official download or service page" aria-label="Open BitLocker official download or service page" data-inta-brand="app-bitlocker"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4538691198fe0ea7526e.png" alt="BitLocker official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [BitLocker](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/security/operating-system-security/data-protection/bitlocker/) recovery material available and obtain authorization first. <a id="step-04"></a>

### 04 · Try the narrow recovery option

- [ ] Use <a href="https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/startup-boot/startup-repair" title="Open Startup Repair official download or service page" aria-label="Open Startup Repair official download or service page" data-inta-brand="app-startup-repair"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4538691198fe0ea7526e.png" alt="Startup Repair official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Startup Repair](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/startup-boot/startup-repair) for a startup fault. If the failure follows a known update or driver change, consider the relevant update removal or restore option after checking its effect on data. Review the result before trying another repair.

<a id="step-05"></a>

### 05 · Reserve manual boot edits for a confirmed diagnosis

- [ ] If boot files or the EFI partition require repair, follow <a href="https://www.microsoft.com/" title="Open microsoft.com official download or service page" aria-label="Open microsoft.com official download or service page" data-inta-brand="site-www-microsoft-com"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4538691198fe0ea7526e.png" alt="microsoft.com official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/)’s procedure for the observed firmware mode, disk layout, and [Windows](https://www.microsoft.com/windows) installation. Have another technician verify the selected partitions. Do not paste generic bootrec, <a href="https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/manufacture/desktop/bcdboot-command-line-options-techref-di" title="Open BCDBoot official download or service page" aria-label="Open BCDBoot official download or service page" data-inta-brand="app-bcdboot"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4538691198fe0ea7526e.png" alt="BCDBoot official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [bcdboot](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/manufacture/desktop/bcdboot-command-line-options-techref-di), <a href="https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-server/administration/windows-commands/diskpart" title="Open DiskPart official download or service page" aria-label="Open DiskPart official download or service page" data-inta-brand="app-diskpart"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4538691198fe0ea7526e.png" alt="DiskPart official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [diskpart](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-server/administration/windows-commands/diskpart), or format sequences.

<a id="step-06"></a>

### 06 · Boot, validate, and preserve a recovery path

- [ ] Test a normal boot and a second restart, confirm encryption is healthy, and run the real business workflow. Record the root cause and boot changes. If recovery remains uncertain, export or image the data before planning a supported reinstall.

<a id="acceptance"></a>

<a id="-prove-the-result"></a>

## Verification

- [ ] The correct [Windows](https://www.microsoft.com/windows) installation boots reliably twice.
- [ ] User data, required applications, and encryption remain accessible.
- [ ] Recovery-key escrow and backup status are confirmed.

> [!WARNING]
> **Pause this route and escalate when:**
> - The recovery key or correct target disk cannot be established.
> - The drive shows physical failure or the proposed action would overwrite the only copy.
