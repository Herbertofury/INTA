## ⚡ Quick answer

<a id="quick-failing-drive"></a>

### 💾 Recover a failing drive

**Stop using the source disk; clicking, physical damage or irreplaceable data needs a recovery-specialist decision first.**

1. Boot a separate <a href="https://www.system-rescue.org/Download/" title="Open SystemRescue official download or service page" aria-label="Open SystemRescue official download or service page" data-inta-brand="systemrescue"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/7f1c7ab41356e0be0354.png" alt="SystemRescue official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [SystemRescue](https://www.system-rescue.org/) USB and identify source and destination by model, serial and size.
2. Follow the <a href="https://ftp.gnu.org/gnu/ddrescue/" title="Open GNU ddrescue official download or service page" aria-label="Open GNU ddrescue official download or service page" data-inta-brand="ddrescue"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/5599e1400b179d1550a0.png" alt="GNU ddrescue official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [GNU ddrescue](https://www.gnu.org/software/ddrescue/) imaging procedure with a persistent mapfile; do not run file-system repairs on the source.
3. Work on an image copy, record unreadable ranges and open the client’s priority files to verify recovery.

**<a href="https://www.system-rescue.org/Download/" title="Open SystemRescue official download or service page" aria-label="Open SystemRescue official download or service page" data-inta-brand="systemrescue"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/7f1c7ab41356e0be0354.png" alt="SystemRescue official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [SystemRescue](https://www.system-rescue.org/)** · <a href="https://www.system-rescue.org/Download/" title="Open SystemRescue official download or service page" aria-label="Open SystemRescue official download or service page" data-inta-brand="systemrescue"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/7f1c7ab41356e0be0354.png" alt="SystemRescue official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Official page](https://www.system-rescue.org/) · **<a href="https://www.system-rescue.org/Download/" title="Open SystemRescue official download or service page" aria-label="Open SystemRescue official download or service page" data-inta-brand="systemrescue"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/7f1c7ab41356e0be0354.png" alt="SystemRescue official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Download x64 rescue image · ISO](https://fastly-cdn.system-rescue.org/releases/13.03/systemrescue-13.03-amd64.iso)**

<a id="quick-deleted-files"></a>

### 📁 Recover deleted files

**Stop writing to the affected disk and check the backup or recycle bin before attempting recovery.**

1. Use healthy media or a preserved image; route an unstable drive to [image-first recovery](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-failing-drive).
2. Run <a href="https://www.cgsecurity.org/wiki/TestDisk_Download" title="Open PhotoRec official download or service page" aria-label="Open PhotoRec official download or service page" data-inta-brand="photorec"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/afa5cd6dbdcd60b65bf1.png" alt="PhotoRec official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [PhotoRec](https://www.cgsecurity.org/wiki/PhotoRec) against that source and send recovered output to a different disk.
3. Open representative recovered files and record missing items; retain the source until acceptance.

## 🧭 Match the symptom

| You see this | Do this next | You should get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The drive clicks, was dropped or repeatedly disconnects** | Stop repeated boots and scans; record symptoms and have the owner choose specialist recovery. | The remaining recoverability is not consumed by routine repair attempts. |
| **A damaged drive remains readable enough to image** | Boot <a href="https://www.system-rescue.org/Download/" title="Open SystemRescue official download or service page" aria-label="Open SystemRescue official download or service page" data-inta-brand="systemrescue"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/7f1c7ab41356e0be0354.png" alt="SystemRescue official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [SystemRescue](https://www.system-rescue.org/), identify source and destination by model/serial/size and follow <a href="https://ftp.gnu.org/gnu/ddrescue/" title="Open GNU ddrescue official download or service page" aria-label="Open GNU ddrescue official download or service page" data-inta-brand="ddrescue"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/5599e1400b179d1550a0.png" alt="GNU ddrescue official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [GNU ddrescue](https://www.gnu.org/software/ddrescue/) with a mapfile on persistent healthy storage. | An image and resumable mapfile preserve readable ranges and identify unreadable gaps. |
| **Files were deleted from healthy media** | Check backups first; otherwise run <a href="https://www.cgsecurity.org/wiki/TestDisk_Download" title="Open PhotoRec official download or service page" aria-label="Open PhotoRec official download or service page" data-inta-brand="photorec"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/afa5cd6dbdcd60b65bf1.png" alt="PhotoRec official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [PhotoRec](https://www.cgsecurity.org/wiki/PhotoRec) against the healthy source or image and save to a separate disk. | Recovered files open successfully; lost names or folder structure are recorded. |
| **The image is encrypted or recovery reports missing ranges** | Obtain the correct recovery material and assess the client’s priority files on a working copy. | Usable files and unrecoverable areas are explicitly distinguished. |

### ✅ Finish the job

- [ ] Source and destination identities are recorded; when imaging was required, the image and mapfile identities are recorded.
- [ ] Priority recovered files open in their actual applications.
- [ ] Any unreadable ranges and missing files are documented before acceptance.

**Change route when:** There is physical damage or an unstable connection that prevents controlled imaging. · A proposed repair would write to the only source or overwrite an unverified destination.

### 🎯 Go straight to a specific fix

- **[Try filesystem undelete before file carving](#fix-recovery-preserve-names-on-ntfs)** — Files were deleted from a healthy NTFS volume and original names or folders matter.
- **[Resume imaging without swapping source and target](#fix-recovery-resume-the-correct-disk-image)** — A recovery session stopped and device names changed after reconnecting or rebooting.
- **[Validate carved files without trusting their filenames](#fix-recovery-carved-files-have-wrong-names)** — <a href="https://www.cgsecurity.org/wiki/TestDisk_Download" title="Open PhotoRec official download or service page" aria-label="Open PhotoRec official download or service page" data-inta-brand="photorec"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/afa5cd6dbdcd60b65bf1.png" alt="PhotoRec official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [PhotoRec](https://www.cgsecurity.org/wiki/PhotoRec) produces numbered folders and files rather than the original directory tree.

> [!NOTE]
> **Start here.** Stop ordinary use of the source drive. Ask whether the data is replaceable and whether a verified backup exists before applying power again.

<a id="-work-the-case"></a>

## Procedure

Work in order, record the result of each step, and change route when the evidence calls for it.

<a id="step-01"></a>

### 01 · Choose professional recovery when needed

- [ ] Clicking, repeated spin-up, liquid or impact damage, unstable detection, and irreplaceable data justify a specialist recovery decision before further attempts. Repeated scans can consume the remaining readable life. Do not open a drive or repeatedly power-cycle it.

<a id="step-02"></a>

### 02 · Identify every device

- [ ] In the <a href="https://www.kernel.org/" title="Open Linux official download or service page" aria-label="Open Linux official download or service page" data-inta-brand="app-linux"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/eda717b4f81bf8bcb609.png" alt="Linux official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Linux](https://www.kernel.org/) rescue session, record source and destination model, serial, capacity, interface, and mount state. Use a known-good cable and stable power when appropriate. Disable automount and keep the source unmounted or read-only; do not initialize, format, or run filesystem repair on it.

**<a href="https://www.kernel.org/" title="Open Linux official download or service page" aria-label="Open Linux official download or service page" data-inta-brand="app-linux"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/eda717b4f81bf8bcb609.png" alt="Linux official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Linux](https://www.kernel.org/) shell (<a href="https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/releases" title="Open INTA Rescue official download or service page" aria-label="Open INTA Rescue official download or service page" data-inta-brand="app-inta-rescue"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/70ad9f0981b05341142b.png" alt="INTA Rescue official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [INTA Rescue](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/blob/main/docs/rescue.md)) · List block devices, model, serial, size and mount points so the source and destination can be identified before imaging.:**

```text
lsblk -o NAME,SIZE,MODEL,SERIAL,TYPE,MOUNTPOINTS
```

> **Before running:** Read-only inventory. Do not run filesystem repair, SMART self-tests or write operations against a failing source before the recovery plan is approved.

**Official command reference:** <a href="https://www.kernel.org/" title="Open Linux official download or service page" aria-label="Open Linux official download or service page" data-inta-brand="app-linux"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/eda717b4f81bf8bcb609.png" alt="Linux official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Linux lsblk manual](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man8/lsblk.8.html)

**Command legend:** <a href="https://www.kernel.org/" title="Open Linux official download or service page" aria-label="Open Linux official download or service page" data-inta-brand="app-linux"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/eda717b4f81bf8bcb609.png" alt="Linux official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Linux lsblk manual](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man8/lsblk.8.html) · [Return to the step context](#step-02).

<a id="step-03"></a>

### 03 · Prepare a separate healthy destination

- [ ] Choose a destination with sufficient capacity for the image and recovery output. Record the exact source, output file or device, and persistent mapfile path. A second technician should verify those identities before any imaging command that can overwrite a destination.

<a id="step-04"></a>

### 04 · Image with a resumable recovery tool

- [ ] Use <a href="https://ftp.gnu.org/gnu/ddrescue/" title="Open GNU ddrescue official download or service page" aria-label="Open GNU ddrescue official download or service page" data-inta-brand="ddrescue"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/5599e1400b179d1550a0.png" alt="GNU ddrescue official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [GNU ddrescue](https://www.gnu.org/software/ddrescue/) following its manual, with a mapfile saved on healthy storage. Start with a low-stress pass that retrieves easy data before any deliberate retry phase. Preserve the mapfile and image together, track errors and temperature, and stop if the device deteriorates.

<a id="step-reconcile-the-image-and-mapfile-before-recovery"></a>

### 05 · Reconcile the image and mapfile before recovery

- [ ] Perform [image and mapfile reconciliation](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-failing-drive) before filesystem work. Record the imaging command, source size, rescue domain, output path, mapfile path and saved status. Use the mapfile to distinguish finished ranges from non-tried, non-trimmed, non-scraped and bad-sector ranges; retain incomplete areas explicitly. A large image file, a finished process or an exit without a fatal error does not prove all source bytes were recovered. Preserve the image/mapfile pair and its acquisition notes together.

<a id="step-protect-the-accepted-acquisition-checkpoint"></a>

### 06 · Protect the accepted acquisition checkpoint

- [ ] After the imaging process has stopped and its writes are complete, record a checksum of the image and preserve the corresponding mapfile version on healthy storage. Hash or verify a working copy from healthy storage before analyzing it. This proves copy identity, not source completeness; do not reread the failing source solely to obtain a comparison hash. If imaging is resumed, retain the earlier checkpoint identity and record the new image/mapfile pair separately.

<a id="step-05"></a>

<a id="05--recover-from-a-working-copy"></a>

### 07 · Recover from a working copy

- [ ] Keep the original recovery image unchanged and create a working copy if capacity permits. Use <a href="https://www.cgsecurity.org/wiki/TestDisk_Download" title="Open TestDisk official download or service page" aria-label="Open TestDisk official download or service page" data-inta-brand="testdisk"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/afa5cd6dbdcd60b65bf1.png" alt="TestDisk official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [TestDisk](https://www.cgsecurity.org/wiki/TestDisk) for partition/filesystem investigation or <a href="https://www.cgsecurity.org/wiki/TestDisk_Download" title="Open PhotoRec official download or service page" aria-label="Open PhotoRec official download or service page" data-inta-brand="photorec"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/afa5cd6dbdcd60b65bf1.png" alt="PhotoRec official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [PhotoRec](https://www.cgsecurity.org/wiki/PhotoRec) for file carving as appropriate. Save recovered files to a different healthy location and expect carving to lose some names or folder structure.

<a id="step-06"></a>

<a id="06--validate-what-was-actually-recovered"></a>

### 08 · Validate what was actually recovered

- [ ] Open representative documents, photos, databases, and archives with the user’s applications. Compare expected folders and counts where known, record unreadable regions and missing files, and retain the recovery image until the client accepts the result. Separate image coverage from file usability: a representative sample is a sample, not proof that every file is intact. For priority databases or archives, use the owning application's validation or [restore test](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-backup-restore) on a copy and report exactly what passed. Include expected but missing folders, failed sample checks and the agreed image-retention decision in the handoff.

<a id="acceptance"></a>

<a id="-prove-the-result"></a>

## Verification

- [ ] The original source has not been repaired or overwritten.
- [ ] The image, mapfile, and device identities are recorded.
- [ ] Recovered files are stored separately and checked for usability.
- [ ] Unreadable data and incomplete recovery are explicitly reported.
- [ ] The recorded mapfile coverage distinguishes successfully copied ranges from every remaining incomplete state.
- [ ] The stopped acquisition checkpoint has an image checksum and its corresponding mapfile; working-copy identity is verified on healthy storage.
- [ ] Priority-file/application tests and sample limits are recorded separately from byte coverage, with unresolved loss stated clearly.

> [!WARNING]
> **Pause this route and escalate when:**
> - The source develops new noises, unstable detection, or worsening read failures.
> - Source and destination cannot be distinguished with certainty.
> - The data value calls for a specialist rather than further bench attempts.

## 🎯 Specific fixes

<a id="fix-recovery-preserve-names-on-ntfs"></a>

### Try filesystem undelete before file carving

**Use when:** Files were deleted from a healthy NTFS volume and original names or folders matter.

**Start here:** Stop writes to the affected filesystem and prepare a separate destination.

1. On a healthy source or preserved working image, use <a href="https://www.cgsecurity.org/wiki/TestDisk_Download" title="Open TestDisk official download or service page" aria-label="Open TestDisk official download or service page" data-inta-brand="testdisk"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/afa5cd6dbdcd60b65bf1.png" alt="TestDisk official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [TestDisk](https://www.cgsecurity.org/wiki/TestDisk)’s documented Advanced > partition > Undelete path for NTFS.

**Prove it worked:** Recovered files are usable, with original metadata retained where the filesystem still provides it.

**If this does not resolve it:** If media is unstable, return to imaging first. No undelete method can promise data already overwritten or discarded.

1. Locate the existing image and persistent mapfile on healthy storage and confirm they belong to this acquisition.
2. Re-read the intended input/output paths and the <a href="https://ftp.gnu.org/gnu/ddrescue/" title="Open GNU ddrescue official download or service page" aria-label="Open GNU ddrescue official download or service page" data-inta-brand="ddrescue"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/5599e1400b179d1550a0.png" alt="GNU ddrescue official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [ddrescue](https://www.gnu.org/software/ddrescue/) command against the acquisition record; never rely on the old letter or device order alone.

**If this does not resolve it:** Do not resume if device identity, mapfile pairing or destination overwrite scope cannot be proved.
