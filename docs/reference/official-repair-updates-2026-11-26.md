## Source conflict resolved

An older <a href="https://www.microsoft.com/" title="Open microsoft.com official download or service page" aria-label="Open microsoft.com official download or service page" data-inta-brand="site-www-microsoft-com"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4538691198fe0ea7526e.png" alt="microsoft.com official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Microsoft connection article](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/fix-problems-connecting-your-google-account-to-the-microsoft-cloud) still describes turning on IMAP inside personal <a href="https://mail.google.com/" title="Open Gmail official download or service page" aria-label="Open Gmail official download or service page" data-inta-brand="web-gmail"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4a3cfc17be3f4e8ad2c8.png" alt="Gmail official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/). The account owner's <a href="https://support.google.com/" title="Open support.google.com official download or service page" aria-label="Open support.google.com official download or service page" data-inta-brand="site-support-google-com"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/4a3cfc17be3f4e8ad2c8.png" alt="support.google.com official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [current instructions](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/7126229?hl=en) say that personal-account IMAP is always on. The new fix follows Google's current account-specific instructions and distinguishes organizational administration from personal settings. It does not copy a stale enable-switch step.
