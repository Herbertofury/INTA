**Start here:** Search existing scans for the exact domain or URL first; if a fresh scan is needed, select its visibility before submission.

**Submission behavior:** Public scans are searchable. Unlisted scans remain visible to vetted Pro subscribers. Private scans are accessible to the submitter or anyone given their unique scan ID.

**Use for:** Inspect redirects, scripts and downloads behind a suspicious link.

**Start here:** Search existing reports first; for a new public URL, inspect its report visibility before submitting.

**Read the result:** A timestamped behavioral report; a completed scan with no alerts does not establish safety.

**Access:** Public report browsing and search are available without sign-in; extra capabilities depend on account permissions. Anonymous submission produces a public report; configurable visibility needs an account.

**Submission behavior:** Public and restricted submissions may reach security partners; private analysis is separate and permission-controlled.
