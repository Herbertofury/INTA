## 🧪 How the picks were chosen

Each pick has an official source for its purpose, a route into an existing service guide, a no-cost use case that fits technician work, and a limit that changes the next action. Commercial subscriptions, cloud storage, support contracts and the operating system itself can still carry a cost.

[WizTree](https://diskanalyzer.com/download) and [WizFile](https://antibody-software.com/wizfile/download) remain useful catalog options. Their [current disk-analyzer terms](https://diskanalyzer.com/eula) and [current file-search terms](https://antibody-software.com/wizfile/eula) reserve free use for personal work, so [WinDirStat](https://github.com/windirstat/windirstat/releases/latest) and the desktop edition of [Everything](https://www.voidtools.com/downloads/) lead these no-cost business scenarios.

### Current independent test evidence

[AV-TEST’s September–October 2026 report](https://www.av-test.org/en/antivirus/home-windows/windows-11/october-2026/microsoft-defender-antivirus-consumer-4.18-261516/) gave [Microsoft Defender Antivirus](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/microsoft-defender-antivirus-windows) Consumer 4.18 on [Windows 11](https://www.microsoft.com/windows/windows-11) **6/6 in protection, performance and usability**. [AV-Comparatives’ July–October 2026 real-world test](https://av-comparatives.org/tests/real-world-protection-test-july-october-2026/), released November 16, placed that product in its **Standard** award tier. Different workloads, time periods and scoring methods matter; neither result establishes a universal winner.

Those are resident-antivirus protection results. They do not rate the standalone cleanup effectiveness of [Microsoft Safety Scanner](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/safety-scanner-download) or [Microsoft Defender Offline](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/microsoft-defender-offline). The choices below use documented capabilities and service fit. We have not established comparable independent ratings for every utility, so no invented stars, overall scores or “highest-rated” badges are assigned.

## Remove viruses

**🛡️ [Microsoft Safety Scanner](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/safety-scanner-download) · [Quick answer](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Remove-viruses) · [Complete procedure](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-malware-removal)**

**First action:** Disconnect a suspected compromised PC from the network before scanning; use the [incident guide](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Contain-ransomware) if encryption is spreading.

1. Record the symptoms and isolate affected connections; keep the existing real-time protection in place.
2. On a trusted PC, download a fresh [Microsoft Safety Scanner](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/safety-scanner-download); transfer it safely and run a Full scan.
3. Save the scan log, review remaining symptoms and persistence, then follow the guide’s cleanup or rebuild decision.

Run a portable, on-demand malware scan without replacing the client’s existing protection.

**Free use:** No-cost Microsoft download; no paid antivirus subscription required.

**Boundary:** Contain spreading compromise first; download a fresh copy for the job and treat a clean scan as limited evidence.

Save the detailed log and follow the guide’s [cleanup decision ladder](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-malware-removal). An unresolved compromise needs the documented escalation or rebuild route.

**Evidence:** Microsoft documents malware detection/removal, a detailed scan log and a 10-day download expiry; this is a workflow-based pick, not a comparative cleanup rating. [Source 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/safety-scanner-download) · [Source 2](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2012/03/22/free-pc-safety-scan/).

## Fix a printer

**🖨️ [Windows Get Help](https://aka.ms/PrinterConnection) · [Quick answer](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Fix-a-printer) · [Complete procedure](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-printer-repair)**

**First action:** Check that the intended printer is powered on, ready and connected before changing its queue or driver.

1. Check paper, ink or toner, displayed errors and the selected printer; compare a device test page if available.
2. Run the printer troubleshooter in [Windows Get Help](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/support/get-help/about-get-help) on the affected PC and review the exact queue or connection finding.
3. Apply the indicated repair, then print a test page and the user’s original document.

Use the supported [Windows](https://www.microsoft.com/windows) printer diagnostic to separate connection, queue and device problems.

**Free use:** Included with supported [Windows](https://www.microsoft.com/windows).

**Boundary:** Scope changes to the affected printer; preserve unrelated queued jobs and use a driver suitable for the PC architecture.

**Evidence:** Microsoft routes [Windows 11](https://www.microsoft.com/windows/windows-11) printing problems through its printer troubleshooter and provides device, connection and test-page checks. [Source 1](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/hardware/printer/fix-printer-connection-and-printing-problems-in-windows) · [Source 2](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/support/get-help/windows-troubleshooters).

**First action:** Check whether one device, one connection or the whole office has lost access.

2. In [Windows Get Help](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/support/get-help/about-get-help), search “connect to network and internet” and run [network diagnostics](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-network-dns).

Start with supported connection diagnostics before escalating to packet capture or network resets.

**Free use:** Included with supported [Windows](https://www.microsoft.com/windows).

**Boundary:** A shared outage needs the network owner; retain VPN and adapter settings before disruptive changes.

**Evidence:** Microsoft documents a Network and Internet diagnostic and an ordered set of Wi-Fi, router and device comparisons. [Source 1](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/support/get-help/windows-troubleshooters) · [Source 2](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/connectivity-networking/fix-wi-fi-connection-issues-in-windows).

## Fix email

**✉️ [Windows Get Help](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/support/get-help/classic-outlook-troubleshooters) · [Quick answer](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Fix-email) · [Complete procedure](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-outlook-email)**

**First action:** Check webmail and identify whether the affected app is classic [Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/outlook) or new [Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/outlook) before changing a profile.

1. Test sign-in and send/receive in webmail; record the exact error and whether other users are affected.
2. For classic [Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/outlook), run the matching [Windows Get Help](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/support/get-help/about-get-help) diagnostic; for new [Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/outlook), use its supported troubleshooting branch.
3. Retest send, receive and the affected shared mailbox or calendar; preserve local-only mail before any profile rebuild.

Use the mail client and service scope to choose the correct authentication, startup or connectivity repair.

**Free use:** Diagnostics are included; email service licensing is separate.

**Boundary:** The classic [Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/outlook) diagnostics do not apply to new [Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/outlook); do not delete a profile before protecting local-only data.

**Evidence:** Microsoft identifies separate classic [Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/outlook) diagnostics and explicitly excludes new [Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/outlook) from those tests. [Source 1](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/support/get-help/classic-outlook-troubleshooters) · [Source 2](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/classic-outlook-not-responding-stuck-at-processing-stopped-working-or-freezes) · [Source 3](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-apps/outlook/administration/architecture-changes-new-outlook) · [Source 4](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/getstarted/outlook-for-windows-not-responding-hangs-freezes-or-stops-working).

For new [Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/outlook), use the [version-specific troubleshooting page](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/getstarted/outlook-for-windows-not-responding-hangs-freezes-or-stops-working). The classic diagnostic link above is deliberately labeled and does not repair every mail client.

## Recover a failing drive

**💾 [SystemRescue](https://www.system-rescue.org/Download/) · [Quick answer](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Recover-a-failing-drive) · [Complete procedure](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-failing-drive)**

**First action:** Stop using the source disk; clicking, physical damage or irreplaceable data needs a recovery-specialist decision first.

1. Boot a separate [SystemRescue](https://www.system-rescue.org/) USB and identify source and destination by model, serial and size.
2. Follow the [GNU ddrescue](https://www.gnu.org/software/ddrescue/) imaging procedure with a persistent mapfile; do not run file-system repairs on the source.
3. Work on an image copy, record unreadable ranges and open the client’s priority files to verify recovery.

The primary download is a ready-to-boot [SystemRescue](https://www.system-rescue.org/) ISO containing [GNU ddrescue](https://www.gnu.org/software/ddrescue/). The imaging steps require an operator who can identify disks and manage a persistent mapfile.

Preserve readable data in an image with a resumable mapfile before attempting file-system repairs.

**Free use:** Free GPL-licensed software for personal and business use; separate destination storage is required.

**Boundary:** Confirm source and destination; stop for physical damage, repeated disconnects or irreplaceable data that warrants a recovery specialist.

Keep the image and mapfile together. The [image completeness check](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-failing-drive) records unreadable ranges, and [recovered-file acceptance](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-failing-drive) checks the client’s actual priorities.

**Evidence:** The GNU manual explains good-area-first recovery, resumable mapfiles and unreadable-range accounting; an image alone does not prove usable files. [Source 1](https://www.gnu.org/software/ddrescue/manual/ddrescue_manual.html) · [Source 2](https://www.gnu.org/software/ddrescue/) · [Source 3](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.html#DoesTheGPLAllowUseOfTheProgramForAnyPurpose).

**Boot environment evidence:** The official [download page](https://www.system-rescue.org/Download/) supplies the ISO and verification files; the [included-tools list](https://www.system-rescue.org/System-tools/) identifies [GNU ddrescue](https://www.gnu.org/software/ddrescue/).

## Recover deleted files

**📁 [PhotoRec](https://www.cgsecurity.org/wiki/TestDisk_Download) · [Quick answer](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Recover-deleted-files) · [Complete procedure](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-failing-drive)**

**First action:** Stop writing to the affected disk and check the backup or recycle bin before attempting recovery.

1. Use healthy media or a preserved image; route an unstable drive to [image-first recovery](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-failing-drive).
2. Run [PhotoRec](https://www.cgsecurity.org/wiki/PhotoRec) against that source and send recovered output to a different disk.
3. Open representative recovered files and record missing items; retain the source until acceptance.

Recover recognizable file contents from healthy media or an image when an intact backup cannot restore them.

**Free use:** Free GPL-licensed software for personal and business use.

**Boundary:** Stop source-disk writes and recover to another disk; original names and folders may be lost, and failing media needs imaging first.

Check an existing backup first. Work from a stable image when needed, then open representative recovered files; a folder full of recovered output is not sufficient acceptance.

**Evidence:** The project documents read-only source access and recovery by file signatures across damaged file systems; recoverability still depends on surviving data. [Source 1](https://www.cgsecurity.org/wiki/PhotoRec) · [Source 2](https://www.cgsecurity.org/wiki/PhotoRec_Step_By_Step) · [Source 3](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.html#DoesTheGPLAllowUseOfTheProgramForAnyPurpose).

## Fix a slow PC

**🐢 [Microsoft Sysinternals Suite](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sysinternals/downloads/sysinternals-suite) · [Quick answer](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Fix-a-slow-PC) · [Complete procedure](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-slow-pc)**

**First action:** Reproduce one slow task and check storage health before applying cleanup or startup changes.

1. Use [Process Explorer](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sysinternals/downloads/process-explorer) to identify the CPU, memory or I/O bottleneck during that task.
2. Inspect the relevant process or startup entry with [Microsoft Sysinternals Suite](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sysinternals/) and make one justified change.
3. Repeat the same task and compare its duration, resource use and responsiveness.

Inspect process activity and startup entries to identify a concrete cause before changing settings.

**Free use:** No-cost Microsoft utilities under the vendor license; obtain them from the official source for the serviced device.

**Boundary:** Choose utilities supported on the client’s [Windows](https://www.microsoft.com/windows) release; observe before terminating processes or disabling startup entries.

Use [Process Explorer](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sysinternals/downloads/process-explorer) for the process question and [Autoruns](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sysinternals/downloads/autoruns) for the startup question; the suite contains both. Capture the observation before changing an entry.

**Evidence:** Microsoft documents process ownership, handles, loaded modules and startup inspection; these observations support a measured troubleshooting decision. [Source 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sysinternals/downloads/sysinternals-suite) · [Source 2](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sysinternals/downloads/process-explorer) · [Source 3](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sysinternals/downloads/autoruns) · [Source 4](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sysinternals/license-terms).

Start a one-time attended [Windows](https://www.microsoft.com/windows) support session with separate screen-sharing and control approval.

**Free use:** Free Microsoft application for the documented support-staff workflow; organization policy still applies.

**Boundary:** Requires internet and a present user; use the organization’s approved managed tool when unattended access or session audit logs are required.

Confirm the helper’s identity through the existing support channel. End the session with the user and record the work in the service ticket; the application does not create local session audit logs.

**Evidence:** Microsoft’s IT guidance documents the session-code flow, helper sign-in, user approval and the absence of local session logs. [Source 1](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/client-management/client-tools/quick-assist) · [Source 2](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9P7BP5VNWKX5).

## Fix a PC that will not boot

**🚀 [Windows Recovery Environment](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/manufacture/desktop/windows-recovery-environment--windows-re--technical-reference) · [Quick answer](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Fix-a-PC-that-will-not-boot) · [Complete procedure](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-boot-recovery)**

**First action:** Record the startup error and confirm disk health and [BitLocker](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/security/operating-system-security/data-protection/bitlocker/) recovery access before attempting repairs.

1. Check power, attached devices and the selected boot disk; stop if storage is failing.
2. Open [Windows Recovery Environment](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/manufacture/desktop/windows-recovery-environment--windows-re--technical-reference) and run [Startup Repair](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/startup-boot/startup-repair) for the identified installation when appropriate.
3. Follow the matching recovery branch, verify a normal boot and open priority files before closing the job.

Use the built-in recovery environment to distinguish [startup repair](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/startup-boot/startup-repair) from data-preserving recovery or reinstall.

**Free use:** Included with licensed [Windows](https://www.microsoft.com/windows).

**Boundary:** Encrypted recovery may require the correct key; do not reset or reinstall before protecting required data.

**Evidence:** Microsoft documents the recovery environment, [Startup Repair](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/startup-boot/startup-repair) and separate causes of boot failure. [Source 1](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/backup-recovery/windows-recovery-environment) · [Source 2](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/client-management/advanced-troubleshooting-boot-problems) · [Source 3](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/backup-recovery/recovery-options-in-windows).

## Diagnose crashes

**🧩 [WinDbg](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/debugger/) · [Quick answer](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Diagnose-crashes) · [Complete procedure](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-hardware-bsod)**

**First action:** Record the stop code, failure time and recent hardware or driver changes before replacing components.

1. Check reliability and event history, available crash dumps and storage health to narrow the failure.
2. For a dump, use [WinDbg](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/debugger/) to inspect the crash evidence; follow the indicated driver, memory or hardware branch.
3. Change one supported cause at a time and repeat the failing workload while checking for new crashes.

Use crash evidence to select the appropriate hardware or driver test.

**Free use:** Free Microsoft debugger.

**Boundary:** Dump analysis is an operator task; a module named in one crash does not by itself prove the root cause.

**Evidence:** Microsoft documents bug-check investigation and [WinDbg](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/debugger/) dump analysis rather than treating every stop error as faulty RAM. ## Contain ransomware

**First action:** Isolate affected connections and alert the incident owner immediately if files are being encrypted or compromise is spreading.

1. Disconnect affected systems from networks and protect reachable backups without destroying useful evidence.
2. Preserve the timeline, ransom note and available logs privately; scope affected accounts and systems with the incident owner.

Contain the incident and protect recovery options before routine cleanup.

**Free use:** No extra download needed for the first response.
