## 🖨️ Printer discovery, drivers and scanning

First read the printer’s model and connection details from its panel or configuration page, then confirm the actual subnet. A scan result is a candidate address; verify the device before changing a queue, driver or network setting. The [printer guide](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-printer-repair) contains the OEM support routes.

| Question | Current choice | Practical boundary |

Its currently published EXE is **2.5.4594.1**; a current availability check does not turn that into a newly released 2026 binary. Limit the range and confirm the device. |
| Use a maintained cross-platform scanner | [Angry IP Scanner](https://angryip.org/) · [Download Windows x64 EXE · Java bundled](https://github.com/angryip/ipscan/releases/download/3.12.2/ipscan-3.12.2-setup.exe) | **3.12.2** setup includes Java. | Need controlled host or service discovery | [Nmap](https://nmap.org/) · [Download Windows setup · EXE](https://nmap.org/dist/nmap-7.993-setup.exe) | Use its [host-discovery controls](https://nmap.org/book/man-host-discovery.html) for a specific question. | Printing works but scanning/ADF does not | [NAPS2](https://www.naps2.com/) · [Download Windows x64 portable · ZIP](https://github.com/cyanfish/naps2/releases/download/v8.6.1/naps2-8.6.1-win-x64.zip) | Stable **8.6.1** is the default. Its linked remote-control product has separate terms. No paid remote-control component is needed merely to identify an address.

## ⚖️ What “best fit” means here

The recommendation weighs the fault, supported platform, setup burden, reversibility, useful output, commercial-use context and an official acquisition route. Release dates and implementation language alone do not establish detection accuracy, recovery success or suitability for a client machine.

The current [AV-Comparatives July–October 2026 report](https://av-comparatives.org/tests/real-world-protection-test-july-october-2026/) tests complete resident protection products. Its results do not rank stand-alone cleanup sessions or establish that one recovery utility is universally best. Scanner selection here follows its documented task; no protection-suite score is reassigned to [Microsoft Defender Offline](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/microsoft-defender-offline), [Microsoft Safety Scanner](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/safety-scanner-download) or [Malwarebytes AdwCleaner](https://www.malwarebytes.com/adwcleaner).

[YARA-X](https://github.com/VirusTotal/yara-x) remains a specialist rules-based inspection tool. [Rustinel](https://github.com/Karib0u/rustinel), [PRX-SD](https://github.com/openprx/prx-sd) and [Sentinella](https://github.com/LeewayOpenSoftware/Sentinella) remain evaluation choices. Their current release availability is recorded, while promotion to a default requires evidence relevant to the job and practical validation.

## ✅ Review evidence and limits

The review preserves the previous 114 catalog IDs and all acquisition URLs, adds four focused tools/features, and retains the existing standalone discovery action alongside the bundled-runtime installer. Six selected/new EXE or ZIP routes returned actual non-HTML bytes in bounded checks; their file headers matched their declared formats. No utility was executed, installed or tested against a client device during this research.

The [current review evidence](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/blob/main/docs/reference/job-tool-review-checks.json) records the checked releases, binary responses, official sources and preservation results. The full catalog keeps the earlier [acquisition evidence](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/blob/main/docs/reference/qol-acquisition-checks.json), including publisher access blocks and mirror-dependent download pages. A successful file response is not a code-signature, efficacy or hardware-compatibility certification. Follow the exact job’s acceptance checks, record the observed result, and review the official options page when a pinned download changes.
