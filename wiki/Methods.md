# 🧠 Methods that turn symptoms into proof

[Home](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki) › **Methods**

**Choose a method by the question it answers.** A tool collects or changes something; a method explains why that action is useful and what evidence should change the next step. Open a linked method to reach its complete field guide.

The registry contains **45 named methods**: a method for each of the **22 job guides**, plus **18 reusable diagnostic concepts** and **five service-evidence methods**. These are <a href="https://herbertofury.github.io/INTA/" title="Open INTA official download or service page" aria-label="Open INTA official download or service page" data-inta-brand="app-inta"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/INTA/main/assets/brand-icons/70ad9f0981b05341142b.png" alt="INTA official page" width="18" height="18" valign="middle" /></a> [INTA](https://herbertofury.github.io/INTA/) operating methods supported by the linked platform sources. They are not a claim that every technique was invented, standardized or independently benchmarked here.

## 🔎 Diagnose the real failure

| Method | When it helps | What proves progress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Evidence-led performance diagnosis](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-slow-pc) | Use for slowness, freezes or hitching with an observable workload. | The same workload improves and required applications, protection and devices still work. |
| [Layered network diagnosis](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-network-dns) | Use when connectivity fails or behaves differently across devices or services. | The original application works through the intended path and adjacent services remain healthy. |
| [Directory trust diagnosis](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-ad-trust) | Use for a domain trust error on a member workstation. | Fresh sign-in and required resources work with a unique active computer identity. |
| [Mail-flow isolation](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-outlook-email) | Use when sending, receiving or a specific mailbox workflow fails. | A message arrives, a reply returns and required archives or shared-mailbox behavior still work. |
| [Print-path isolation](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-printer-repair) | Use when printing fails or output is wrong. | Internal and application test pages pass without breaking other users or queues. |
| [Crash and hardware isolation](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-hardware-bsod) | Use for stop errors, unexpected restarts, overheating or reproducible instability. | The original failure scenario and the relevant diagnostics have recorded outcomes. |

## 🧩 Narrow the uncertainty

| Method | When it helps | What proves progress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Scope isolation](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-intake-triage) | Use when the symptom is vague or reported as affecting everything. | A failing and a working comparison establish the next investigation boundary. |
| [Known-good comparison](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-intake-triage) | Use when an interchangeable component can distinguish likely causes. | The compared conditions and the result for both paths are recorded. |
| [Single-change experiment](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-slow-pc) | Use when several explanations could fit the current evidence. | The predicted result, actual result and rollback are recorded before another variable changes. |
| [Reproducible baseline](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-slow-pc) | Use before claiming a repair or performance improvement. | Another technician can repeat the same workload and compare equivalent observations. |
| [Timestamp correlation](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-hardware-bsod) | Use for intermittent crashes, update failures or incidents. | Timezones, clock uncertainty and the supporting sequence are recorded. |
| [DNS-versus-connectivity isolation](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-network-dns) | Use when an address works but a name fails, or different resolvers give different results. | The failing layer is identified without replacing organization-specific DNS blindly. |

## 🧾 Close the [service evidence](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Service-Evidence) gap

| Method | When it helps | What proves progress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Remote capability preflight](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-remote-support) | Before choosing or starting a one-time or managed support connection. | Both platforms, user presence, permissions, transfer route and reconnect plan are recorded and the required capabilities are exercised. |
| [Temporary access revocation](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-remote-support) | At the end of remote work or when ownership and scope change. | Session end, closed channels and removed credentials/enrollments are recorded, or continuing access has a named owner and review date. |
| [Scan coverage reconciliation](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-malware-removal) | After each malware scan and before a cleanup or escalation decision. | The case has scan mode, version, scope, completion, exclusions or errors, findings and disposition with the product report. |
| [Clean rebuild acceptance](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-malware-removal) | When the trusted endpoint baseline cannot be established or the incident owner requires rebuilding. | Pre-wipe target and data decisions, media provenance, patch/protection state, restore checks, account-response ownership and customer-task evidence are recorded. |
| [Image and mapfile reconciliation](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Job-failing-drive) | At an imaging checkpoint and before filesystem repair or extraction from a working copy. | Saved mapfile states, remaining unreadable ranges, matching image/mapfile checkpoint and healthy-storage copy checks are recorded separately from file usability. |

Use [Service Evidence](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Service-Evidence) for the concrete acceptance records and [Tool Selection](https://github.com/Herbertofury/INTA/wiki/Tool-Selection) for diagnostic and remote-support choices.
