## Checks that prevent clutter

- Every service guide belongs to exactly one existing layout group; cross-links provide other routes.
- A tool has one stable catalog ID, one acquisition record and one primary category. Editions and platform files are acquisition alternatives.
- Add a specific fix to the existing service guide before creating another overlapping guide. Split only for a genuinely different job and preserve old routes.
- New candidates carry source evidence, platform and business-use limits. Source review is not runtime certification; preview candidates remain labeled.
- Only a substantive source review advances a review date. A successful link check or rebuild does not refresh guidance.
- Keep an actionable research queue for every job and tool category; use severity first and oldest reviewed area next to avoid neglect.
- Preserve existing IDs, commands, sources, cases and saved state; rebuild and verify the source, live hub, actual Wiki and offline copy together.

The build rejects duplicate tool identities/names, unexplained duplicate official product pages, unknown references, missing service homes, empty fixes, invalid sources and gaps in the research queue. The normal build and Wiki checks also enforce exact acquisition actions and generated-page consistency.
